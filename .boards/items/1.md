The mode transition now registers correctly — the ember rides exactly on the tear
(`--burn` is a `<length>`, so the SVG mask circle and the ember gradient agree) and
the rim is displaced again via `.burn-ember-warp`. It still does not convince.

## What is wrong

**The line is too evenly circular.** `#burn-displace` uses a single `feTurbulence`
octave pair at one frequency, so every part of the front wobbles by the same amount
at the same scale. Real burn fronts are not uniform: they run ahead where the paper
is thin and stall elsewhere, so the outline goes lopsided, not evenly crinkled. The
current edge reads as "a circle with texture applied" rather than as a shape fire
made.

**It should be slower still.** `BURN_MS` is 1800.

## Directions worth trying

- Break the circle's *low* frequencies rather than adding more high-frequency
  crinkle. A second, much coarser turbulence (or a low `baseFrequency` layer mixed
  in via `feComposite`/`feBlend` before the displacement) would pull whole arcs of
  the front in and out. More `numOctaves` will not do this — it adds fine detail,
  and detail is not the problem.
- Consider driving the mask with a distorted shape instead of a circle: an
  `feTurbulence` fed through `feDisplacementMap` at low frequency and high scale,
  or a hand-authored blobby `<path>`, so the base geometry is already irregular.
- The front could also advance non-uniformly in *time* per direction, which a
  single radius cannot express — that likely means abandoning `r`-on-a-circle.

## Constraints (all load-bearing — see DESIGN.md §5, §6)

- `filter` runs before `mask`. The ember's filter must stay on `.burn-ember-warp`;
  moving it onto `.burn-ember` masks a flawless circle out of a warped gradient.
- Whatever drives the hole and the ember must share one unit, or the rim drifts off
  the tear again.
- Cost is per-frame over a full-viewport filter region. Extra turbulence octaves are
  a noise sample per pixel per frame; prefer a second cheap layer over more octaves,
  and keep the end radius bounded (currently `82vmax`).
- Nothing underneath the clone may animate a layout property.
- `BURN_MS` in `App.jsx` is the only duration; CSS reads it as `--burn-dur`.

Consequence of the decision to **publish tiers rather than quote every engagement**
(see [`docs/editing-without-tina.md`](../../docs/editing-without-tina.md) §9). A price
someone can act on without a conversation implies a path from "saw the price" to
"working site" that involves nobody.

This is a business surface, not a feature, and it is the largest single addition to the
plan. None of it exists.

## What it needs

- **Signup + account creation.** Supabase Auth handles the mechanics; what does not
  exist is anything that turns a new account into a provisioned site.
- **Billing.** Stripe is already a dependency in `LiveSpiritSeedsMk2/marketplace` with a
  working checkout and webhook, so the plumbing has a precedent to lift — but
  subscriptions, plan changes, dunning and cancellation are all new.
- **Provisioning.** A new customer needs a site row, a starting template, a subdomain,
  and a working editor login, with no human in the loop.
- **Seat cap enforcement.** The add-on is per site with a cap of ~3 editors. This needs
  `site_members` to be authoritative. **Refuse the fourth invite with an upgrade path —
  never silently degrade an existing editor**, which is the failure mode that generates
  support tickets and ill will at the same time.
- **Plan → capability mapping.** One place that answers "does this site have editing?"
  Scattering that check is how a downgraded account keeps a paid feature.

## What this changes elsewhere

- **Multi-tenancy moves to phase 0.** The plan's §2 previously called it a non-goal; that
  is now superseded. `sites` and `site_members` are in the schema for this reason.
- **[Item 6](6.md) becomes blocking.** A self-serve customer chooses a structure with
  nobody advising them. Three skeletons covering a hero and one band is not something a
  stranger can build a business on, and there is no conversation in which to warn them.
- **Support scales with signups.** Today every client arrived through a conversation,
  which is a natural throttle. Published tiers remove it, and the first month where
  signups outpace the ability to answer email is a predictable one.

## Open before starting

**How self-serve is self-serve?** Prices can be published while onboarding stays manual
— a "get started" form that reaches a person. That is a legitimate and much cheaper
version of this epic, and it captures most of the filtering benefit of published pricing.
Deciding which is being promised changes the size of this item by months.

**`/keeper` currently argues against its own pricing table.** The closing copy says the
chooser is the easy part and the real work is everything after — an argument for quoted
project fees. It has to change when numbers go on the page.
